ABOUT

Marceli Mierzwicki is an independent designer working across industrial and product design, furniture, lighting, mobility, and research. He begins by looking closely at how an object is used, understood, and made. Material, structure, and technology become tools for questioning familiar forms and finding clearer solutions. Moving from everyday objects to larger systems, his practice balances analytical thinking with a distinctive visual language. Each project develops through observation and experimentation, with careful attention to proportion, function, and detail. The aim is to make work that feels useful, individual, and considered.
