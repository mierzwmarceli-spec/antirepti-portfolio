01 / INDUSTRIAL DESIGN

SCREWDRIVER

Multifunctional hand tool

PORTFOLIO / 2026 01 — 05

01 / OVERVIEW

An experimental hand tool combining ratcheting, telescopic reach and directly accessible bit storage.

Designed for constrained workspaces, the tool prioritises one-handed usability, fast access and ergonomic control. Instead of relying on rubberised coatings, it investigates how geometry itself can generate tactile interaction.

GEOMETRY AS GRIP

Sectional variation, surface rhythm and sculpted finger indexing replace soft rubber. The handle becomes the ergonomic system, designed for stable control during repetitive mechanical work.

DIRECT ACCESS

Externally accessible bits reduce the actions needed to change tools. Ratcheting and telescopic reach are condensed into the core structure, keeping function visible rather than concealed.

04 / DEVELOPMENT

From FDM PLA to SLA resin, prototypes refined grip, tolerances and surface quality. Translucency exposes the inner mechanism, making usability and construction the source of the object’s identity.
